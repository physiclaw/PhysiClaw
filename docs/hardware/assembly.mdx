PhysiClaw's hardware uses a [CoreXY](https://corexy.com/) motion system. Two motors working
in tandem drive the gantry, and a solenoid drives the stylus tip on Z to tap. The whole assembly
takes **about three hours**.

The assembly manual has the complete, clear steps. Read it online or download the PDF. You can use
[FreeCAD](https://www.freecad.org/) to view the [3D assembly model (STEP)](/downloads/physiclaw_assembly_3d.zip).
Before you start, browse the hardware gallery to
get a feel for the whole rig, or watch the [demo video](https://youtu.be/YJ1O6gW0KVw) to see it in action; the
[assembly video](https://youtu.be/Acmta1kzEko) walks through the whole build.

:::caution[A few things to get right during assembly]
The manual covers these, but they're worth emphasizing:

- **Earth-ground the stylus tip**: connect it to bare metal on the back of a grounded PC chassis, never a wall
  socket, never the board GND.
- **Position the camera**: open your system's built-in webcam preview and confirm the phone screen
  sits flat in frame, fills as much of it as possible, with no tilt or distortion.
- **Keep A/B belt tension equal**: mismatched tension makes the arm move inaccurately.
- **Lock down the motor pulleys**: apply threadlocker to the set screws. The
  motors constantly reverse, and the repeated direction changes can work the screws loose. Once a
  pulley slips, motion starts to drift.
:::
