---

PhysiClaw's hardware favors standard, off-the-shelf parts, which are easy to source, low-cost, and
reliable. The custom parts are 3D-printed.

A full set of parts costs around **US$300**, excluding shipping. Before ordering, read through
the assembly manual to fully understand the hardware. When purchasing, check each item against the
bill of materials to make sure nothing is missing and quantities are correct. Any omission means
another order and more time lost to shipping.

Parts are available from [AliExpress](https://www.aliexpress.com), with custom parts ordered from
3D-printing services. Shipping availability and prices vary by region.
