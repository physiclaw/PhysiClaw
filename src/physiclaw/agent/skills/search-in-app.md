# Search in App

Copy the query, then tap the search field's placeholder row rather than its camera, microphone, or AI-search icon. Shopping home-page bars often open a separate search page: re-ground the actual input there.

Clear stale entered text (dark text or a clear ✕); gray placeholder text disappears on paste. Long-press the field, then ground Paste from the resulting view. Only a learned Paste box permits bundling across that popover. Once grounded, Paste and search/return may share a sequence.

Verify that the results header contains your query. Submitting an empty field searches its rotating ad placeholder; tapping a suggestion searches that suggestion. A mismatched query means the paste missed: correct the input before continuing.
